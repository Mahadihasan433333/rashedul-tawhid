Biography

Behind the Viewfinder

Rashedul Tawhid is an internationally acclaimed Director of Photography with over a decade of experience crafting high-impact visual narratives. Known for his painterly use of natural key light, bold composition, and deep technical mastery of both vintage optical glass and cutting-edge sensor technology.

Based in Dhaka & available for global assignments across Asia, Europe, and North America. Member of the International Cinematographers Guild (ICG).

"Every frame must serve the emotional core of the script. Technique without story is just noise."
